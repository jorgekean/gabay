# GABAY - User Guide & Process Flow

GABAY (Guidance & Anecdotal Behavior Analytics Yield) is an Early Warning System and digital logbook designed to help schools track student behavior, intervene early for at-risk students, and ensure strict compliance with the Data Privacy Act.

The system is designed to work completely **offline**, meaning you can use it even when the school's internet connection drops. It will seamlessly sync data once the connection is restored.

---

## 1. Teacher & Adviser Workflow
Teachers are the frontliners of the school. Their primary goal is to log behavioral incidents and positive anecdotes quickly without dealing with physical logbooks.

### Key Features for Teachers:
- **Digital Anecdotal Ledger**: Teachers can securely search for a student and log an incident (e.g., Truancy, Bullying, Outstanding Leadership). 
- **Offline Reliability**: Even if the Wi-Fi is down in the classroom, teachers can still log incidents. The app saves it locally and syncs it later.
- **SARDO (At-Risk) Alerts**: When viewing their class roster, teachers will see red `AT RISK (SARDO)` badges automatically appear next to students who meet the drop-out warning thresholds (e.g., 3 absences or multiple offenses).
- **Status Tracking**: Teachers can view a student's behavioral timeline and track the status of an incident they reported (e.g., seeing it move from "Open" to "Resolved"). 
- **Strict Privacy**: Teachers *cannot* see sensitive psychological notes written by the Guidance Counselor.

### The Process:
1. The teacher witnesses an incident in the classroom.
2. They open GABAY, click **"Log Incident"**, select the student, categorize the behavior, and type a brief description.
3. The incident is instantly saved and flagged as "Open" for the Guidance Counselor to review.

---

## 2. Guidance Counselor Workflow
The Guidance Counselor is responsible for triage, intervention, and confidential case management. 

### Key Features for Counselors:
- **The Digital Vault**: A highly restricted area of the system. Counselors have exclusive access to a secure text area on every incident where they can type private psychological assessments and therapy notes.
- **Case Takeover**: Counselors can take over any "Open" incident reported by a teacher, update its status to "Under Investigation" or "Resolved", and schedule Parent Conferences.
- **4Ps Subsidy Protection**: Counselors are alerted earlier for 4Ps beneficiaries. The system uses stricter thresholds for these students to ensure interventions happen *before* they lose their government financial assistance.

### The Process:
1. The Counselor reviews the Triage Dashboard at the start of the day and clicks on "Open" incidents.
2. They call the student in for a session.
3. While reviewing the student's profile, the Counselor clicks **"Update Case / Notes"**.
4. They type their private therapy notes into the **Digital Vault** and change the status to "Resolved".
5. The teacher sees the incident is "Resolved", but the private therapy notes remain completely hidden from everyone except the Counselor.

---

## 3. Principal & Administrator Workflow
The Principal needs a high-level view of the school's behavioral health to make data-driven decisions and generate compliance reports for DepEd.

### Key Features for Principals:
- **Triage Analytics Dashboard**: An immediate, visual overview of the school. The Principal can see which specific Grade Levels have the highest spike in offenses for the current month.
- **Priority Watchlist**: A dedicated section on the dashboard highlighting all students currently flagged as SARDO, allowing the Principal to follow up with advisers.
- **DPA-Compliant Redacted Reporting**: The ability to generate physical reports for PTA meetings or DepEd audits without violating student privacy.

### The Process:
1. The Principal checks the Dashboard to see real-time graphs of behavioral trends across different grade levels.
2. During a PTA meeting, the Principal needs to present recent bullying trends.
3. They go to the Incidents Page, filter by "Bullying", and click **"Print Redacted"**.
4. The system automatically strips out full names and replaces them with initials (e.g., `Juan Dela Cruz` becomes `J. D. C.`).
5. The Principal safely prints and distributes the report, fully compliant with the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173).
